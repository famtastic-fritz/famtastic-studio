## What to carry forward
- **Lane self-verification is not integration verification.** Both the frontend lane and the gates lane reported success on evidence that was consistent with a broken page (one curled HTML and got 200, the other saw h1_count=0 and attributed it to incomplete work by others). The orchestrator running the full suite is what closed it. Keep that step explicit at every milestone.
- **A 404 on a page module renders as an honest-looking empty page.** That is the honest-states rule's blind spot: truthful-looking emptiness can still be a bug. The smoke assertions (exactly one h1, no page errors) are the counterweight and should grow, not shrink.
- Settings renders the paths registry as raw JSON. Honest but crude; Phase 2's per-screen critique pass should fix it.
